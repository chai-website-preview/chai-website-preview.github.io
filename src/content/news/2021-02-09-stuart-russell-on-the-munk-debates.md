Stuart Russell debated Melanie Mitchell, Professor at the Santa Fe Institute, in the latest episode of The Munk Debates, a debate series on major policy issues.

In the episode, titled The Rise of Thinking Machines, Prof. Russell argued for the question, “Be it resolved, the quest for true AI is one of the great existential risks of our time.”
