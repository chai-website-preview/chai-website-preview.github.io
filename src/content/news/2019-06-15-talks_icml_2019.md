CHAI faculty and graduate students presented their papers at the latest International Conference on Machine Learning.

Rohin Shah, Noah Gundotra, Pieter Abbeel, and Anca Dragan gave a 5 minute presentation on their paper [On the Feasibility of Learning, Rather than Assuming, Human Biases for Reward Inference](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2019/ScheduleMultitrack?event=4979) along with a poster session. Micah Carroll, Rohin Shah, Tom Griffiths, Pieter Abbeel, and Anca Dragan, along with outside researchers Mark Ho and Sanjit Seshia, gave a poster presentation at the I3 Workshop for [On the Utility of Learning about Humans for Human-AI Coordination](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/137uRyohdH8ZvnGz_vP7HpwVGHN8nLHLw).
