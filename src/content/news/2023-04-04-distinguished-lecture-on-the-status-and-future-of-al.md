Four distinguished lectures on the Status and Future of Al organised by [CITRIS Research Exchange](https://citris-uc.org/news-events/citris-research-exchange/) and BAIR will be livestreamed and held in person at CHAI.

- 4/5: Stuart Russell, Professor of Computer Science, UC Berkeley, on How Not To Destroy the World With AI
- 4/12: Alison Gopnik, Distinguished Professor of Psychology, UC Berkeley
- 4/19: Michael I. Jordan, Distinguished Professor of EECS and of Statistics, UC Berkeley
- 4/26: Pamela Samuelson, Richard M. Sherman, Distinguished Professor of Law, UC Berkeley
