Two new papers co-authored by CHAI members appear on arXiv.

CHAI researchers and collaborators Paria Rashidinejad, Banghua Zhu, Cong Ma, Jiantao Jiao, and Stuart Russell posted [Bridging Offline Reinforcement Learning and Imitation Learning: A Tale of Pessimism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.12021) on arXiv.

Current and former CHAI members including Paul Knott, Micah Carroll, Anca Dragan, and Rohin Shah co-authored the paper [Evaluating the Robustness of Collaborative Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05507) alongside Sam Devlin, Kamil Ciosek, and Katja Hofmann.
