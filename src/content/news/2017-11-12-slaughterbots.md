CHAI’s principle investigator Stuart Russell in conjunction with the Future of Life Institute released [Slaughterbots](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9CO6M2HsoIA), a short-film highlighting the dangers of armed micro-drones with swarm intelligence being used for targeted assassinations and terrorist attacks. The film created a vast amount of media coverage, with major newspapers such as The Guardian covering it and generated discussions at major think-tanks such as the [Center for New American Security](https://www.cnas.org/publications/commentary/debating-slaughterbots-and-the-future-of-autonomous-weapons). More can be read about it [here](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2017/nov/13/ban-on-killer-robots-urgently-needed-say-scientists).
