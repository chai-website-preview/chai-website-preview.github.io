Founders Pledge, a non-profit organisation where entrepreneurs make a commitment to give a percentage of their proceeds when they sell their business, has recommend CHAI as an impactful donation opportunity.
In their article, they discuss existential risk as a cause area to donate to, including the risks of misaligned artificial intelligence. The article can be found [here](https://founderspledge.com/stories/existential-risk-executive-summary).
