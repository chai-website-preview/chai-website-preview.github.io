Professor Tom Lenaerts joins CHAI as a Visiting Scholar from the Computer Science Department of the Université Libre de Bruxelles.

Prof. Lenaerts and CHAI researcher Michael Dennis will investigate the relationship between translucent players and commitment devices, considering an evolutionary game theory perspective. They will collaborate with Elias Fernandez Domingos and Ines Terrucha from Vrije Universiteit Brussel.
