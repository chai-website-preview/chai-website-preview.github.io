CHAI Assistant Director Rosie Campbell gave a talk at the San Francisco AI Meetup to explain in basic terms what a neuron is, how they combine to form a network, why convolutional neural networks have had so much attention, and the challenges and opportunities of AI progress. See the slides [here](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1zCIyJvDHBJUBKxjVnoxV902HiIcytOO8i2_MCFYEEYE/edit#slide=id.g190d1a4eab_0_103) and a write-up of an earlier version of the talk [here](https://medium.com/@RosieCampbell/demystifying-deep-neural-nets-efb726eae941). Rosie also sat down for a chat with tech blogger Mia Dand on [“Keeping AI Safe and Beneficial for Humanity”](https://becominghuman.ai/keeping-ai-safe-and-beneficial-for-humanity-4d0416300dfa). Their discussion covered topics such as CHAI’s mission and approach, the problem of AI alignment, and why you might or might not believe AI safety research is important.
