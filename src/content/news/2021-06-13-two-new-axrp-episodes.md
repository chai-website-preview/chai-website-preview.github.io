CHAI PhD student Daniel Filan’s podcast AXRP published two new episodes.

[Episode 8](https://axrp.net/episode/2021/06/08/episode-8-assistance-games-dylan-hadfield-menell.html) features Dylan Hadfield-Menell, former CHAI student and current MIT professor, in a discussion on assistance games. [Episode 9](https://axrp.net/episode/2021/06/24/episode-9-finite-factored-sets-scott-garrabrant.html) features MIRI researcher Scott Garrabrant in a discussion on finite factored sets. Find the episodes on the [AXRP website](https://axrp.net/) or any podcast provider.
