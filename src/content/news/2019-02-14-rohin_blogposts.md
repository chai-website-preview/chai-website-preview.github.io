CHAI grad student Rohin Shah published two blog posts on his recent paper [Preferences Implicit in the State of the World](https://openreview.net/forum?id=rkevMnRqYQ). The first was [Learning Preferences by Looking at the World](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2019/02/11/learning_preferences/), published on the Berkeley AI Research (BAIR) Blog. The [second blog post](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/7f6DNZhracD7RvxMr/learning-preferences-by-looking-at-the-world) was an article of the same name that was published on the AI Alignment Forum. This article went into more of the details and background of the original paper.
