CHAI faculty and affiliates presented the following papers at [AAAI 2021](https://aaai.org/Conferences/AAAI-21/):

- [Probabilistic Dependency Graphs](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.10800.pdf) by Oliver Richardson, Joseph Y. Halpern at Cornell University
- [Asking the Right Questions: Learning Interpretable Action Models Through Query Answering](https://aair-lab.github.io/Publications/vms_aaai21.pdf) by Pulkit Verma, Shashank Rao Marpally, and Siddharth Srivastava at Arizona State University
- [Unifying Principles and Metrics for Safe and Assistive AI](https://aair-lab.github.io/Publications/srivastava_aaai21.pdf) by Siddharth Srivastava
