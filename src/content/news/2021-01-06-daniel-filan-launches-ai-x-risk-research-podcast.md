In December 2020 CHAI PhD candidate Daniel Filan launched the [AI X-risk Research Podcast](https://axrp.net/) (AXRP, pronounced axe-urp).

In each episode, Daniel interviews the author of a paper and discusses how it might reduce the existential risk of AI. In the first three episodes, Daniel features CHAI researchers Andrew Critch, Rohin Shah, and Adam Gleave.

Follow AXRP on any podcast provider and [Twitter](https://twitter.com/AXRPodcast).
