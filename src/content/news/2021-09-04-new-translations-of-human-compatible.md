The following translations of Stuart Russell's 2019 book *Human Compatible: Artificial Intelligence and the Problem of Control* have been recently released.

- [İnsanlık için Yapay Zekâ: Yapay Zekâ ve Kontrol Problemi](https://www.buzdagikitabevi.com/insanlik-icin-yapay-zek-yapay-zek-ve-kontrol-problemi). Translated to Turkish by Barış Satılmış. Ankara: Buzdağı Yayınevi, 2021.
- [어떻게 인간과 공존하는 인공지능을 만들 것인가](http://www.gimmyoung.com/Book/BookView?bookCode=BC003343&bookType=all). Translated to Korean by Lee Han-eum. Seoul: Gimm-Young, 2021.
- [AI新生](https://www.msz.co.jp/book/detail/08984/). Translated to Japanese by Nobuhiko Matsui. Tokyo: Misuzu Shobo, 2021.
- [Сумісний з людиною. Штучний інтелект і проблема контролю](https://bookchef.ua/product/sumisniy-z-lyudinoyu-shtuchniy-intelekt-i-problemi-kontrolyu/). Translated to Ukrainian by Viktoria Zenhyea. Kiev: Bookchef, 2020.
