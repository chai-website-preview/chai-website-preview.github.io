Professor Pieter Abbeel recently launched a new podcast, [The Robot Brains](https://www.therobotbrains.ai/), where he hosts leading experts in AI robotics.

In the first episode Andrej Karpathy, director of AI at Tesla, joins Pieter to discuss training driverless cars with machine learning and working with Elon Musk. Olga Russakovsky, Princeton professor and co-founder of AI4All, examines AI data’s diversity problem in the second episode. Follow the show on any podcast service.
