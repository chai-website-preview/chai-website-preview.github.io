CHAI PhD student Rachel Freedman will present two papers at a workshop at IJCAI 2020. The first paper, [“Choice Set Misspecification in Reward Inference,”](http://ceur-ws.org/Vol-2640/paper_14.pdf) is coauthored with CHAI Professor Anca Dragan and PhD student Rohin Shah. The paper analyzes what happens when a robot inferring reward functions from human feedback makes incorrect assumptions about the human’s choice set. The second paper, [“Aligning with Heterogeneous Preferences for Kidney Exchange,”](http://ceur-ws.org/Vol-2640/paper_15.pdf) addresses the problem of preference aggregation by AI algorithms in a real-world public health context: kidney exchange. The paper suggests a roadmap for future automated moral decision making on behalf of heterogeneous groups.
