Adam Gleave and Rohin Shah attended the 2018 [GoalsRL Workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/goalsrl) and presented the paper [Active Inverse Reward Design](https://gleave.me/papers/2018-active-ird.pdf) and Adam additionally presented a paper on [Multi-task Maximum Entropy Inverse Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08882). Also, Daniel Filan presented the paper [Exploring Hierarchy-Aware Inverse Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.05037).
