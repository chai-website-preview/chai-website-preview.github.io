CHAI researchers Michael Dennis, Adam Gleave, Cody Wild, Neel Kant, and Stuart Russell, along with Sergey Levine, gave a talk on their paper [Adversarial Policies: Attacking Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.10615) at the International Conference on Machine Learning 2019. There is a video of the talk on the [ICML Github](https://icml2019workshop.github.io) (starts at 1h:35m) and the slides can be [here](https://slideslive.com/38916739/security-vulnerabilities-of-machine-learning-systems)
