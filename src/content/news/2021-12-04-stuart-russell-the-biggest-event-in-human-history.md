In his first BBC Reith Lecture, Stuart Russell asks how artificial intelligence could transform the world - and discusses whether fears of AI are well founded or whether we can learn to make it work for us? Stuart Russell is the first computer scientist to be selected as a BBC Reith Lecturer. Russell delivered four lectures across the UK to discuss his views on AI. You can listen to the lecture (or read a transcript) [here](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m001216j).
