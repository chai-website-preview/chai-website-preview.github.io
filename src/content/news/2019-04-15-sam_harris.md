Professor Stuart Russell and Professor Alison Gopnik were on a recent episode of Sam Harris’ Making Sense podcast. The two CHAI PIs were interviewed to discuss their contributions to John Brockman’s new anthology, [Possible Minds: 25 Ways of Looking at AI](https://www.amazon.com/Possible-Minds-Twenty-Five-Ways-Looking/dp/0525557997). You can listen to the podcast on [Sam Harris’ website](https://samharris.org/podcasts/153-possible-minds/).
