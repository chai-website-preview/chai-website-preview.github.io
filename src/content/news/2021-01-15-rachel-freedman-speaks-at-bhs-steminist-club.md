On January 11 2021, Rachel Freedman [spoke](https://bhssteminist.wixsite.com/club/post/guest-speaker-rachel-freedman) at the Berkeley High School STEMinist Club on the topic of human-compatible AI.

She introduced the King Midas Problem, defined human-compatible AI, and discussed her research developing a kidney exchange algorithm to align with human values.
