Stuart Russell participated in Transformation in Action: Positive Futures, a [plenary](https://www.weforum.org/events/global-technology-governance-summit-2021/sessions/imagining-optimistic-tech-futures) in the World Economic Forum 2021 Global Technology Governance Summit.

The event, titled Transformation in Action: Positive Futures, brought together AI experts, industry futurists, and writers to discuss future outcomes of technology governance.
