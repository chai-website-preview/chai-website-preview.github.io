Stuart Russell participated in Debates on the Future of Robotics Research, a virtual workshop at ICRA 2021.

In [Debate 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q79SJnEvbfE), Russell argued for the resolution “Individual success metrics prevalent in robotics create perverse incentives that harm the long-term needs of the field.” CHAI alum Jaime Fernández Fisac served as General Co-Chair on the organizing team. Watch recordings of the debates at [roboticsdebates.org](https://www.roboticsdebates.org/).
