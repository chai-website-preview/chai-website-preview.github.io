Stuart Russell and Rosie Campbell were quoted in the piece [“The Case for Taking AI Seriously as a Threat to Humanity”](https://www.vox.com/future-perfect/2018/12/21/18126576/ai-artificial-intelligence-machine-learning-safety-alignment). It was featured in Vox’s Future Perfect section, which focuses on covering high impact causes such as climate change, AI safety, and animal welfare. The article covers everything from the basics of what AI is to the arguments for why AI could pose a danger to humanity.
