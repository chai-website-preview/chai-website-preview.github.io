Professor Stuart Russell gave a talk to the Netherlands Ministry of Foreign Affairs on the risks of autonomous weapons. Professor Russell has been involved in bringing awareness to the risks that autonomous weapons have, calling them the [New Weapons of Mass Destruction](http://www.the-security-times.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/ST_Feb2018_Doppel-2.pdf) due to their ability to scale with a small number of human operators.
