CHAI’s PI Stuart Russell published “Autonomous Weapons: The New Weapons of Mass Destruction?” in The Security Times. In the piece, he discusses the implications and safety concerns of autonomous weapons, comparing them to weapons of mass destruction due to their scalability, cost-effectiveness, and ability to become fully autonomous. The article can be found [here](https://www.securityconference.de/fileadmin/MSC_/2018/Dokumente/Security_Times_Feb2018.pdf).
