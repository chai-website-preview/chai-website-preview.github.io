CHAI published a [2020 Progress Report](/progress-report) which highlights some of the most important research that CHAI has produced since its inception.

The report also documents our contributions beyond the AI community including contributions to public awareness of the risks from AI systems, contributions to world leaders’ awareness, and connecting with China. Lastly, the report lays out our future plans for the next few years.
