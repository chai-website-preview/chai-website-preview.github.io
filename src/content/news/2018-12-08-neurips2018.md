CHAI graduate students Rohin Shah, Adam Gleave, Daniel Filan, and Dylan Hadfield-Menell, along with interns Matthew Rahtz and Aaron Tucker, attended the [NeurIPS 2018 Conference](https://neurips.cc/), where they presented papers, met with researchers, and learned about work in the rest of the field.

Aaron and Adam presented [Inverse reinforcement learning for video games](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.10593) at the [Deep RL workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/deep-rl-workshop-nips-2018/home), while Rohin presented The [Implicit Preference Information in an Initial State](https://openreview.net/forum?id=rkevMnRqYQ&noteId=rkevMnRqYQ) at the [Deep RL and Learning by Instruction workshops](https://sites.google.com/view/lbi2018/).
