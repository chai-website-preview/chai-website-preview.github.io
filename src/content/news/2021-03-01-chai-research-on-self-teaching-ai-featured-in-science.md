A new article in Science, [Who needs a teacher? Artificial intelligence designs lesson plans for itself](https://www.sciencemag.org/news/2021/01/who-needs-teacher-artificial-intelligence-designs-lesson-plans-itself), features two recent papers from members of CHAI.

The first [paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/hash/985e9a46e10005356bbaf194249f6856-Abstract.html), “Emergent Complexity and Zero-shot Transfer via Unsupervised Environment Design,” by Michael Dennis, Stuart Russell, and Andrew Critch, was presented in the Deep RL Workshop at NeurIPS 2020. The second [paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/hash/2051bd70fc110a2208bdbd4a743e7f79-Abstract.html), “A Novel Automated Curriculum Strategy to Solve Hard Sokoban Planning Instances,” was also presented at NeurIPS 2020 and is co-authored by Prof. Bart Selman of Cornell University. Read more about their work in [Science](https://www.sciencemag.org/news/2021/01/who-needs-teacher-artificial-intelligence-designs-lesson-plans-itself).
