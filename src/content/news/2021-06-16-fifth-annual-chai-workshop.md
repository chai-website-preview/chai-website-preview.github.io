On June 7-8, CHAI held its fifth annual workshop.

Over 150 attendees met virtually on Zoom and in Gather.town to discuss AI safety and beneficial systems. Participants included professors, students, industry researchers, and policymakers from interdisciplinary fields. Here is the [program](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1GaEFnSY8aVY2mjFgq7In6EkDFKw75owz7P_aqloMddM/edit#gid=2047802425) for reference.
