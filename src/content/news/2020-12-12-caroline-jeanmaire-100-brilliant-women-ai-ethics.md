Caroline Jeanmaire, Director of Strategic Research and Partnerships at CHAI, has been recognized as one of [100 Brilliant Women in AI Ethics in 2021](https://100brilliantwomeninaiethics.com/the-list/of-2021/).

The list is published annually by Women in AI Ethics (WAIE), a project of Social Good Fund and Lighthouse3, a technology advisory firm based in Oakland, California. Its mission is to make AI more diverse and accessible, in part by recognizing rising stars in the field.

Congratulations to Caroline, and thank you for your brilliant dedication to making AI safe and beneficial for all!
