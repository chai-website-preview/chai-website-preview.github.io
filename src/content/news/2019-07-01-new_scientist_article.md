A recent [New Scientist article](https://www.newscientist.com/article/2207324-ai-learns-to-gamble-illogically-like-humans-to-predict-our-behaviour/) features a [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.09397) that Tom Griffiths and Stuart Russell wrote along with David D. Bourgin, Joshua C. Peterson, and Daniel Reichman. The article discusses how the researchers were able to make a machine learning model that took into account human biases, like risk adversion, that are usually hard for computer systems to model.
