Thomas Krendl Gilbert coauthored the article [Hard Choices in Artificial Intelligence](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0004370221001065), which will appear in the journal Artificial Intelligence.

The paper explores the problem of technical misspecification, tying it to the philosophical concept of vagueness, before proposing an analytic frame for reorganizing AI pipelines to address this through new forms of stakeholder feedback.
