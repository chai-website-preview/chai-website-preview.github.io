Scott received his PhD at UC Berkeley and was advised by Stuart Russell. He is interested in both the theory and practice of AI alignment. Scott has helped characterize how [RLHF can lead to deception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17747) when the AI sees more than the human, develop [multimodal attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.00236) and [benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10260) for [open-ended agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03279), and use mechanistic interpretability to find [evidence of learned look-ahead](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00877) in a chess-playing neural network. Learn more at his [website](https://scottemmons.com/).
