Anca Dragan got her B.Sc. in Computer Science from [Jacobs University Bremen](https://www.jacobs-university.de/) in Germany, after which she went to [Carnegie Mellon](https://www.cmu.edu/) for her Ph.D. in Robotics. She is now an Assistant Professor in the [EECS Department](https://eecs.berkeley.edu/) at UC Berkeley. Her goal is to enable robots to work with, around, and in support of people. She runs the [InterACT Lab](http://interact.berkeley.edu/), which focuses on algorithms for human-robot interaction—algorithms that move beyond the robot’s function in isolation, and generate robot behavior that also accounts for interaction and coordination with end-users. She also helped found and serves on the steering committee for the [Berkeley AI Research (BAIR) Lab](http://bair.berkeley.edu/).

You can read more about Prof. Dragan’s work and accomplishments at her [website](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~anca/).
