Jun works to study and reduce risks from advanced AI systems. He has spent time as a researcher at New York University working on language model safety, and at CHAI he will be working with Dan Hendrycks to investigate power-seeking behaviour in AI agents.

Prior to his current research interests, Jun spent some years building algorithms for autonomous vehicle sensor calibration at Motional, and studied Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Imperial College London.
