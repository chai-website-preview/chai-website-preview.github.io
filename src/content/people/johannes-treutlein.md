Johannes is an incoming MSc student in computer science at the University of Toronto. Previously, he studied
mathematics and computer science at the Technical University of Berlin and the University of Oxford. His research
interests include multi-agent learning, game theory, bargaining and decision theory. At CHAI, he is working with
Jakob Foerster and Michael Dennis on whether and how cheap talk can help with zero-shot coordination in cooperative
multi-agent RL. Before that, Johannes was a research assistant at the machine learning lab at his university. His
website is [johannestreutlein.com](https://johannestreutlein.com).
