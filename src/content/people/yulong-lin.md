Yulong studies Computer Science at the University of Cambridge, where he has obtained a starred first. For his dissertation, he worked with Professor Michael Bronstein and Professor Pietro Liò to benchmark dynamic graph neural networks and develop new models. Previously, he has interned at Amazon Web Services (AWS) and the National University of Singapore (NUS). He is interested in ensuring that AI is developed safely. At CHAI, he works with Scott Emmons to develop robust models in response to unrestricted adversarial examples.
