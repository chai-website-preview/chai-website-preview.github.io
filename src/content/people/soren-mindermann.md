Sören is a DPhil student in CS in the Applied and Theoretical Machine Learning Group at Oxford University (OATML). His interests in machine learning include its economic and political properties, how it scales, and safety.

Before joining OATML, Sören worked on reward inference and machine learning for game theory with David Duvenaud and Roger Grosse at Toronto’s Vector Institute, at the Future of Humanity Institute at Oxford, and with CHAI. He has degrees in machine learning (UCL), maths (Amsterdam) and Future Planet Studies (Amsterdam).

His website is [oatml.cs.ox.ac.uk/members/soren_mindermann/](https://oatml.cs.ox.ac.uk/members/soren_mindermann/).
