Erik completed his physics undergraduate degree in 2020 and is now a Master’s student in artificial intelligence at the University of Amsterdam. He is passionate about Effective Altruism and wants to use his career to help ensure that AI has a positive impact. At CHAI, he will be working with Adam Gleave on improving the interpretability of reward functions. This could help evaluate learned reward functions when no ground truth is available. Before his CHAI internship, Erik did research on graph-based segmentation, causality, and on equivariant deep learning.
