Sergei is a Master student at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Lausanne (EPFL) studying Computer Science
and Neuroscience. Sergei did an internship at Google which resulted in [a paper on causal models in reinforcement
learning](https://causalrlworkshop.github.io/program/cldm_8.html). Sergei co-founded [the local AI safety group in
Lausanne](https://robustlybeneficial.org/) which does research and discussions. At CHAI, Sergei is working on
adversarial policies with Adam Gleave.
