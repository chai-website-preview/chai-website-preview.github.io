Pavel is a Master’s student at the Technical University of Munich, focusing on Artificial Intelligence. His research interests include Deep Reinforcement Learning and making AI safe and robust. At CHAI he works with Adam Gleave to find defenses for adversarial policies.

He previously worked as a student research assistant at Fraunhofer AISEC on cognitive security technologies. For more information, please visit his [website](https://pavelcz.github.io/).
