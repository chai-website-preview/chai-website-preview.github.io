Adam’s research focuses on reward specification for reinforcement learning. Most recently, Adam developed the [EPIC distance](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.13900.pdf) to compare reward functions. Previously, he has worked on reward modeling techniques with a number of collaborators, including [inverse reinforcement learning (IRL) from vision](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.10593) and [multi-task IRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08882). Adam is also interested in the robustness of reinforcement learning, and has demonstrated the existence of [adversarial policies](http://adversarialpolicies.github.io/) in multi-agent environments: policies that cause an opponent to fail despite behaving seemingly randomly.

Prior to joining Berkeley, Adam did a M.Phil. with Christian Steinruecken and Zoubin Ghahramani in the [Machine Learning Group](http://mlg.eng.cam.ac.uk/) at the University of Cambridge. You can learn more about Adam’s work at his [website](https://gleave.me/) and by following him on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/argleave).
