[Jonathan Stray](http://jonathanstray.com/) is a Senior Scientist at the Center for Human Compatible AI at UC Berkeley, where he works on the design of recommender systems for better personalized news and information. He previously [taught](http://www.compjournalism.com/) the dual masters degree in computer science and journalism at Columbia University, built [several](https://workbenchdata.com/) [pieces](https://www.overviewdocs.com/) of software for investigative journalism, worked as an editor at the Associated Press, and developed graphics algorithms for Adobe. He holds an MSc in Computer Science from the University of Toronto and an MA in Journalism from the University of Hong Kong.
