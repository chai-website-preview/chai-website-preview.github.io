Brian Judge is an AI Policy Fellow at CHAI, where he studies the governance of artificial intelligence and its implications for global political economy. He received his PhD in Political Science from the University of California, Berkeley. His work has appeared in *New Political Economy*, *New Media & Society*, and the *Cambridge Journal of Economics* and his first book is forthcoming from Columbia University Press. You can read more about Brian’s work at his [website](https://brianjudge.com/).
