Tania Lombrozo received her Ph.D. from Harvard University and is a Professor of [Psychology at UC Berkeley](https://psychology.berkeley.edu/). She will be joining the [Department of Psychology](https://psych.princeton.edu/) at Princeton University in July 2018. Her research aims to address basic questions about learning, reasoning, and decision-making using the empirical tools of experimental psychology and the conceptual tools of analytic philosophy. You can read more about Prof. Lombrozo’s work [here](https://psychology.berkeley.edu/people/tania-lombrozo) or [here](https://psych.princeton.edu/person/tania-lombrozo).
