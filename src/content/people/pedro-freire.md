Pedro is a M.Sc. student in Mathematics and Computer Science at Ecole Polytechnique, France. Some of his AI interests
include (i) how to make agents better at processing higher-level feedback from humans, and (ii) the development of tools
for RL interpretability – in particular to better understand how we can reduce variance and increase sample-efficiency
in learning, and have agents with better guarantees of generalization.Other of his interests include interactive theorem
proving, software tooling, game development, virtual reality, and human-computer interaction more broadly.

Previously, he worked at Google developing tools for monitoring and performing statistical analysis on the behavior of
YouTube video classifiers, and worked at Facebook on the development of Augmented Reality software. He is currently
working on benchmarks specialized in evaluating properties of Inverse Reinforcement Learning algorithms.
