Charlotte is a PhD student at the University of Warwick at the center for Mathematics for Real-world Systems. In her
PhD research, she uses game theory to study how people respond to intelligent traffic systems such as route planning
apps and traffic lights. She is also interested in cooperative multiagent reinforcement learning. At CHAI, Charlotte
is working on finding safe beliefs that encourage cooperation in sequential social dilemmas. Her broad interests include
AI safety, fairness, and bounded rationality.
