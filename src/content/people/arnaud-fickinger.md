Arnaud began his PhD at UC Berkeley in 2019 and is supervised by Stuart Russell. He is interested in multiagent systems and is currently working on adding a social choice flavor to assistance games and creating complex behaviors via multiagent interaction.

Arnaud received his MS in Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Visual Computing and his BS in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science from the École Polytechnique in Palaiseau, France. He is currently a Visiting Researcher at Facebook AI and has been a Visiting Research Fellow at Harvard University as well as a Software Engineering Intern at Leia Inc. in Menlo Park.
