Beth is a researcher at OpenAI. Previously she worked as Research Assistant to Chief Scientist at DeepMind. Beth participated in CHAI’s first internship cohort in 2017, where she implemented and proved properties of IRL/IRD algorithms.

Beth earned a degree in Computer Science at the University of Cambridge. At Cambridge she cofounded Future of Sentience (FuSe), a student society affiliated with CSER focused on improving the long-run future. FuSe created an All-Party Parliamentary Group advocating for representation of future generations in the UK Parliament.
