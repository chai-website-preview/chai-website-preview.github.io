Dorsa Sadigh is an Assistant Professor in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering at Stanford University. Her research interests lie in the intersection of robotics, learning, and control theory. Specifically, she is interested in developing algorithms for safe and adaptive human-robot interaction. Dorsa has received her doctoral degree in Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences (EECS) from UC Berkeley in 2017, and has received her bachelor’s degree in EECS from UC Berkeley in 2012. She is awarded the NSF CAREER award, the AFOSR Young Investigator Program Award, the IEEE TCCPS early career award, the Google Faculty Award, and the Amazon Faculty Research Award. Learn more at her [website](https://dorsa.fyi/).
