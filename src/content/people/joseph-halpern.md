**CHAI honors the memory and contributions of former CHAI faculty member Joseph Halpern.** Prof. Halpern was a Professor of [Computer Science](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/) at Cornell University. His research focused on the interface between game and decision theory and computer science, reasoning about knowledge and uncertainty, and causality. His work also shaped conversations in security, fault-tolerant distributed computing, and modal logic.

You can read more about Prof. Halpern’s work and accomplishments on his [website](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/halpern/). Please take a moment to read his obituary [here](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/02/joe-halpern-towering-computer-scientist-and-mentor-dies-72).
